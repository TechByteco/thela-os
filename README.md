## 💡 The "Crazy" Moonshot
Instead of treating India's 10 million informal vegetable and fruit pushcarts (*thelas*) as unorganized chaos to be cleared or replaced by quick-commerce dark stores (Zepto/Blinkit), **ThelaOS transforms them into an intelligent, decentralized, zero-emission urban supply swarm**.

Equipped with any standard ₹6,000 Android smartphone suspended on a bamboo pole above the cart, **ThelaOS** provides:
1. **Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision:** Scans produce in crates, segments 40+ native Indian vegetable varieties, and calculates remaining shelf-life hours from cellular turgor.
2. **Gemini Live Indic Voice Agent:** Semi-literate vendors speak naturally in regional dialects (Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu). Zero typing required.
3. **P2P Swarm Barter Radar:** By 4:30 PM (the daily heat distress cliff), nearby carts within 500m trade complementary surplus/deficit items (e.g., swapping ripe tomatoes for dry onions) without cash friction.
4. **Mandi Drop Flash Engine:** Broadcasts dynamic, geo-fenced evening markdowns to nearby apartment societies via WhatsApp with simulated UPI QR payments.
5. **Janta Evaporative Cooler:** A ₹240 low-tech burlap & clay wick design that adds 3–4 hours of shelf life.

## 🌟 22 Comprehensive Platform Features

1. **Live Camera & Real Image Upload Mode:** Switch between simulated carts, real device webcam (`getUserMedia`), or upload any produce photo for real-time bounding box analysis.
2. **Web Audio Sound Effects (SFX) Synthesizer:** Zero-dependency audio feedback for laser scan sweeps, button clicks, barter match chimes, Mandi Drop alerts, and UPI payment soundbox ringtones.
3. **Real Speech-to-Text Microphone Input:** Native browser speech recognition (`webkitSpeechRecognition`) supporting live voice queries in Hindi and English.
4. **Live Mandi Ticker (Agmarknet APMC Rates):** Rolling market ticker bar displaying real-time wholesale rates and arrival shifts from Azadpur (Delhi), Vashi (Mumbai), and Koyambedu (Chennai).
5. **Direct Sunlight & High-Contrast Night Market Modes:** Specialized high-contrast display mode (black/amber on yellow) engineered for 10,000+ lux midday Indian sun.
6. **Bilingual UI Localization (English ⟷ Hindi [हिंदी]):** Instant full-app toggle switching navigation, metrics, buttons, and tooltips between English and conversational Hindi.
7. **UPI QR Code & Soundbox Voice Payment Simulator:** Generates dynamic BharatPe/Google Pay/Paytm QR codes with authentic voice announcements (*"Google Pay par ₹52 prapt hue!"*).
8. **Real WhatsApp Deep Link Sharing:** Generates clickable `https://api.whatsapp.com/send?text=...` links pre-filled with bilingual Mandi Drop flyers ready to send to real residential WhatsApp groups.
9. **Interactive Custom Inventory Creator:** Modal allowing vendors to add custom items (Aloo, Pyaaz, Bhindi, Gobi, Kela) with custom costs, weights, and automated decay timers.
10. **Live Micro-Climate & Weather Telemetry:** City climate selector (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata) dynamically adjusting produce decay multipliers based on heat and humidity.
11. **Barter Radar Demand Heatmap Overlay:** Toggleable geospatial demand overlay highlighting high-density residential buyer zones on the 2D market map.
12. **Persistent Transaction Ledger & Daily Earnings:** LocalStorage-backed financial ledger tracking executed barters, Mandi Drop sales, total kilograms saved, and net rupee margin retained.
13. **Gemini Live Persona & Voice Speed Selector:** Switch Gemini's persona (*"Chalak Vyapaari"*, *"Polite Mentor"*, *"Telemetry Only"*) with adjustable speech rates (0.85x to 1.2x).
14. **Janta Cooler (Evaporative Cold-Storage) Physical Guide:** Low-tech, zero-electricity physical blueprint (dual jute liner, clay pot wick, ventilation tarp) lowering crate temperatures by 6°C–8°C.
15. **Export Pitch Deck to Printable PDF / Presentation Mode:** High-resolution formatted view with clean print stylesheets for generating 6-slide competition decks for IIT Delhi judges.
16. **Interactive 3D Thela Hardware Blueprint:** Exploded vector diagram displaying the anatomy of an AI-enabled pushcart (bamboo smartphone rig, 10W flexible solar panel, soundbox, digital scale).
17. **Soundbox Hardware Spec Sheet:** Bill of Materials (BOM) detailing the ₹1,380 (~$16) complete retrofit package.
18. **Judge FAQ & Deep-Dive Defense System:** Searchable accordion answering hard questions: offline mesh networking, fraud prevention, vendor adoption, and unit economics.
19. **PWA Manifest & Offline Storage Support:** Fully configured `manifest.json` for home-screen installation on Android devices.
20. **Interactive Guided Demo Tour (Walkthrough Mode):** 6-step guided walkthrough highlighting key modules sequentially in 60 seconds.
21. **Kaggle Competitions Submission Dossier:** Dedicated submission documentation and executable Python pipelines for the user's active Kaggle competitions in `kaggle/`.
22. **Clean Git Repository & Public GitHub Deployment:** Deployed to GitHub under `TechByteco/thela-os`.
